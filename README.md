# Panuvi

**Pan** stands for “for everyone.” **UVI** stands for **Useful, Verifiable Intelligence**. Our goal is intelligence that is useful to people, with impact that can be verified.

Our first step is [**InclusionMed**](https://github.com/panuvi/inclusionmed), an open evaluation initiative to assess AI capabilities across diverse healthcare roles and care settings, with the goal of making high-quality healthcare accessible and beneficial to everyone.
